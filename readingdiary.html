Books that I have been reading

I want to keep track of my reading, and have created this reading diary to do so. This is, in part, a way to encourage myself to finish books I start, especially while I still have the free time to read entire books. The kick-off date for this diary is 1 August 2026, and I plan to update it irregularly.

Sputnik Sweetheart by Haruki Murakami — August 2026

Japanese lesbians just get so lonesome, I could cry. I read this book on holiday with my dad sitting by Lake Annecy in the garden of the Abbaye de Talloires.

Dying Animal by Phillip Roth — August 2026

Beautiful book about a dirty old man. I bought this book in Geneva, when I had 6 hours to kill there and didn't want to talk to any Swiss. I finished it on the way back on the aeroplane, sometimes worried that people would read over my shoulder.

The Napoleon of Notting Hill by G.K. Chesterton — August 2026

Bizarre old book, which I read on the sofa of Thames Rowing Club on a hot day.

England, England by Julian Barnes — August 2026

Very strong first chapter, but let down by extended Euro-satire that feels irrelevant now.

The Map and the Territory by Michel Houellebecq — September 2026

Very fun read, and another book that touches on the subject of dirty old men - in this case, Michel Houellebecq himself.
