Books I've been reading

I want to keep track of my reading. I have done this in part to encourage me to finish books that I start, especially while I still have free time. This diary starts in August 2026, and I plan to update it irregularly.

Sputnik Sweetheart by Haruki Murakami — August 2026

Loney Japanese lesbians. I read this book on holiday with my dad sitting by Lake Annecy in the garden of the Abbaye de Talloires.

Dying Animal by Phillip Roth — August 2026

Beautiful book about being an old pervert, and being a young patriot. I bought this book in Geneva, when I had 6 hours to kill there and didn't want to talk to any Swiss. I finished it on the way back on the aeroplane, sometimes worried that people would read over my shoulder.

The Napoleon of Notting Hill by G.K. Chesterton — August 2026

Bizarre old book, which I read on the sofa of Thames Rowing Club on a hot day.
